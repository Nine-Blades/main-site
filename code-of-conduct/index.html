Initiating an Investigation

When someone reports behavior that may violate the Amtgard Code of Conduct to anyone other than government officers, they should be redirected to report to their local or kingdom officers instead. After any necessary recusals are made, those officers then appoint from amongst themselves an Investigation Lead to collect information for the officers to review.

Depending on the scope of the behavior, the preference for who should be approached is as follows (in order):

If the reported behavior took place at the local level and does not affect other chapters:

  1. Local Monarch
  2. Local Chancellor or Local Guildmaster of Reeves
  3. Other Local Officer

If the reported behavior took place at the kingdom or interkingdom level, or involves local officers:

  1. Kingdom Monarch
  2. Kingdom Chancellor or Kingdom Guildmaster of Reeves
  3. Other Kingdom Officer
  4. Board of Directors member
