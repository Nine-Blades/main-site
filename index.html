What is Amtgard?

Amtgard is a medieval combat sport and Live Action Roleplaying (LARP) game — foam sword fighting (also called boffer combat) mixed with quests, roleplay, and crafting. How do we differ from the LARPs you've seen on TV? In this guide, we'll explain everything you need to know!
